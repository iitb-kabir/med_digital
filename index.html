After 6 Months — ₹500 Per Update

After the free period, any maintenance request is charged at a simple flat rate of ₹500 per issue or update. Examples include:

ℹ️

A Note on Server Scaling

Your clinic website is initially built and hosted for standard clinic-level usage. This comfortably handles the daily traffic of a typical clinic or specialist practice.

In the event your clinic grows significantly — for example, reaching very high daily traffic (around 10,000+ daily active users), the server infrastructure may require upgrades. If this becomes necessary, we will communicate transparently about any additional cloud or server costs required, which will depend entirely on the actual infrastructure needed at that time.

For the vast majority of clinics, this will never be a concern.
